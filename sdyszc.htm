一句话概括

树洞不需要注册、不需要登录，你可以完全匿名地留言。我们不会收集你的姓名、手机号、邮箱、通讯录或位置，也不投放广告、不接入任何统计与分析 SDK。但为了让留言能被展示、并防止有人刷屏，服务器会保存你写下的内容，以及与这条留言关联的 IP 地址和客户端标识。你写的字是公开的，请只写你愿意被陌生人看到的内容。

1. 我们是谁、本政策覆盖什么

树洞是由个人开发者运营的匿名留言板，仅通过 Google Play 分发的 Android 应用提供。本政策说明应用在你使用「浏览留言、发布留言、回复、按日期查找、切换语言」这些功能时，会收集、使用、保存哪些信息。

应用为一次性付费买断（$1.00），购买与退款完全由 Google Play 处理。启动时应用会通过 Google Play 的许可服务校验购买状态；我们不会接触、也不会收集你的支付卡号、账单地址、购买账号或任何财务信息，只接收「是否已授权」这一校验结果。

应用只与自有接口通信：https://787980.xyz/treehole/api.php（根域名不可用时自动回退 https://www.787980.xyz/treehole/api.php）。全部请求走 HTTPS，应用已声明 usesCleartextTraffic="false"，不允许明文 HTTP；数据库账号与密码不会打包进 APK。

3. 我们不收集什么

  • 没有账号体系：不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号、密码、身份证号，也没有登录状态。
  • 不请求通讯录、相机、麦克风、照片/文件、精确或粗略位置、短信、电话、日历、生物识别等权限。
  • 应用只申请两个权限：android.permission.INTERNET（联网）与 com.android.vending.CHECK_LICENSE（向 Google Play 校验购买许可，不读取设备信息、不上传数据）；不使用 WebView，不读写设备外部存储，不申请后台运行或自启动权限。
  • 不含任何广告 SDK、统计分析 SDK、崩溃上报 SDK、推送 SDK、第三方登录或社交分享 SDK，也不含广告标识符（GAID/AAID）采集。
  • 不收集支付信息：一次性购买由 Google Play 结算，我们收不到卡号、账单地址或支付账号，应用只读取 Google Play 的授权校验结果。也不收集健康数据、生物特征，不做跨应用或跨网站追踪，不建立用户画像。

应用使用的 AndroidX AppCompat / RecyclerView / SwipeRefreshLayout、OkHttp、Gson 均为界面与网络解析库，本身不向我们或第三方上传你的个人信息。

4. 我们如何使用与审核这些信息

  • 展示留言、回复与相对时间，提供按日期检索和分页。
  • 用 IP 做短时间防刷，避免机器刷屏或反复提交举报影响其他人的阅读体验。
  • 提供应用内举报：长按留言或回复可选择「举报垃圾或骚扰」「举报违法或有害内容」，记录写入服务端供人工审核处理。
  • 提供本机屏蔽：「不再显示这条」只保存在你的设备上，不上传、不影响他人。
  • 对违法、侵权、骚扰或其他违规内容进行隐藏或锁定（后台管理），必要时依法配合有权机关。
  • 不会把你的内容用于模型训练、广告投放或自动化决策。

5. 我们是否与第三方共享

我们不会出售、出租或向第三方共享你的留言内容、IP 地址或客户端标识，也不会用于广告。

需要说明的基础设施：站点经由 Cloudflare 提供 CDN 与反向代理，因此网络请求会经过其网络（其自身隐私政策适用于该传输环节）；留言数据保存在本服务自有的数据库中，不对外提供接口查询。若法律法规要求或为保护他人人身财产安全所必需，我们可能在必要范围内披露相关信息。

6. 数据保存多久、如何删除

留言、回复及其关联的时间、IP 与客户端标识保存在服务端数据库中，没有自动过期删除机制，会一直保留到管理者将其删除为止。删除设备上的应用不会删除已经发布到服务器的留言。

举报记录（内容 id、类别、IP、客户端标识、时间）同样保存在服务端，处理完毕后由管理者归档或删除。你在本机设置的语言偏好与屏蔽清单只存在设备本地，卸载应用即随之清除。

由于没有账号，应用内不提供自助删除入口。如需删除某条留言或回复，请发送邮件到 260358@qq.com，邮件中注明留言的发布时间、昵称和正文片段以便定位；核实后我们会删除该内容及其回复。

请注意：留言是公开的，任何人都可以阅读、截图或复制。我们只能删除本服务数据库中的副本，无法收回他人已经保存的内容，也无法保证搜索引擎或第三方缓存中的副本。因此发布前请确认你愿意让它被公开。

7. 安全措施

  • 全站强制 HTTPS/TLS，应用禁止明文流量；服务端对参数做长度与格式校验。
  • 数据库凭据只存在于服务端，不会写进应用安装包。
  • IP 与客户端标识不在客户端展示，也不随列表接口返回。
  • 互联网传输不存在绝对安全，我们会持续改进保护措施，但无法承诺零风险。

8. 儿童隐私

树洞不面向 13 岁以下儿童（或你所在地区法定最低年龄以下的未成年人），也不会有意收集其个人信息。应用没有年龄门槛，但我们建议未成年人在监护人陪同下使用。若你是监护人并认为孩子发布了内容，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们会尽快核实并删除。

9. 第三方链接

应用内不嵌入第三方内容；唯一的外部跳转是正版验证未通过时打开本应用在 Google Play 的详情页。本政策页面中指向使用条款与邮箱的链接除外。

In short

Treehole requires no sign-up and no login; you can post completely anonymously. We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts or location, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs. To display posts and prevent spam, the server does store what you write, together with the IP address and a client identifier attached to that post. Everything you write is public: only post what you are comfortable with strangers reading.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Treehole is an anonymous message board operated by an individual developer and available only as an Android app distributed through Google Play. This policy explains what information is collected, used and retained when you browse posts, publish a post, reply, search by date or change the app language.

The app is a one-time $1.00 purchase handled entirely by Google Play, including refunds. On launch the app verifies the purchase through Google Play's licensing service; we never receive or collect your card number, billing address, purchase account or any other financial information — only the licensed / not-licensed result.

The app talks only to our own endpoint: https://787980.xyz/treehole/api.php (falling back to https://www.787980.xyz/treehole/api.php if the apex domain is unreachable). All traffic uses HTTPS, the app declares usesCleartextTraffic="false", and database credentials are never bundled into the APK.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts: no name, email, phone number, password, government ID, and no login state.
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos/files, precise or coarse location, SMS, calls, calendar or biometrics.
  • The app requests only two permissions: android.permission.INTERNET for networking and com.android.vending.CHECK_LICENSE to verify the purchase with Google Play (it reads no device data and uploads nothing). There is no WebView, no external-storage access and no background-start permission.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, push, third-party login or social-sharing SDKs. No advertising ID is collected.
  • No payment data: the one-time purchase is billed by Google Play, so we never receive card numbers, billing addresses or payment accounts; the app only reads the licence result. No health or biometric data, no cross-app tracking and no user profiling.

AndroidX AppCompat / RecyclerView / SwipeRefreshLayout, OkHttp and Gson are used only for UI, networking and JSON parsing; they do not upload your personal information to us or to third parties.

4. How we use and moderate the information

  • To display posts, replies and relative times, and to provide date search and pagination.
  • To apply short-term IP rate limiting so spam or repeated reports do not spoil the board for everyone.
  • To provide in-app reporting: long-press a post or reply to report spam/harassment or illegal/harmful content; the report is stored on the server for human review.
  • To provide on-device blocking: "Hide this content" is stored only on your device, is not uploaded and does not affect others.
  • To hide or lock unlawful, infringing, harassing or otherwise prohibited content, and to cooperate with competent authorities where legally required.
  • Your content is never used for model training, ad targeting or automated decision-making.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent or share your posts, IP address or client identifier with third parties, and we do not use them for advertising.

Infrastructure note: the site uses Cloudflare as a CDN / reverse proxy, so requests pass through its network (Cloudflare's own privacy policy applies to that transit). Post data is kept in our own database and is not exposed through any public query API. We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect someone's safety or rights.

6. Retention and deletion

Posts, replies and their associated timestamps, IP addresses and client identifiers are kept in the server database with no automatic expiry, until removed by the operator. Uninstalling the app does not delete posts already published to the server.

Report records (content ID, category, IP, client identifier, timestamp) are kept on the server and archived or deleted by the operator once handled. Your language preference and hidden-content list live only on the device and disappear when you uninstall the app.

Because there are no accounts, there is no in-app self-service deletion. To request removal of a post or reply, email 260358@qq.com with the timestamp, nickname and a short excerpt so we can locate it; we will delete the item and its replies after verification.

Please note: posts are public and anyone may read, screenshot or copy them. We can only remove the copy in our own database; we cannot retract copies others have saved, nor guarantee removal from search engines or third-party caches. Only post what you are willing to make public.

7. Security

  • HTTPS/TLS everywhere, cleartext traffic disabled; server-side length and format validation on all inputs.
  • Database credentials exist only on the server and are never shipped inside the app.
  • IP addresses and client identifiers are not shown in the app and are not returned by the list API.
  • No transmission over the internet is absolutely secure; we keep improving our safeguards but cannot guarantee zero risk.

8. Children's privacy

Treehole is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age applicable in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. There is no age gate, but minors should use the service with a guardian. If you are a guardian and believe a child has posted content, contact us at the address below and we will verify and remove it promptly.

9. Third-party links

The app embeds no third-party content; its only external navigation opens this app's Google Play listing when licence verification fails. The external references on this page are the Terms of Use and our email address.

10. Changes to this policy
